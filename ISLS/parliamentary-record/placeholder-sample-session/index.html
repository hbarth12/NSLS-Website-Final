Transcript

00:00:12 Speaker of the Assembly: The session is open. Today's agenda has one item: the first reading of draft law No. 14/2026 on the general state budget for fiscal year 2027.

00:00:45 Member A (Damascus): Thank you, Mr. Speaker. The draft allocates 31% of spending to salaries and only 9% to investment. I ask the government to explain how it expects to fund reconstruction on these numbers.

00:02:10 Member B (Aleppo): I support referring the draft to the Budget Committee, but the committee should publish its report at least 7 days before the second reading, on 28 October 2026.

Studio commentary

00:03:05 Studio presenter: We are leaving the chamber briefly. For viewers just joining: this is the first reading, so no vote on the substance of the budget is expected today.

00:03:40 Studio presenter: Under the internal rules adopted on 30 July, a first reading ends with a vote on referral to committee only.

00:04:02 Speaker of the Assembly: I now put the referral to a vote.

(The draft law is referred to the Budget Committee by majority vote.)

00:04:30 Speaker of the Assembly: The session is adjourned until Tuesday at 11:00.
